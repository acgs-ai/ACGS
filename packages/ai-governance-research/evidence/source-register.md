## Claim discipline

Avoid unsupported phrases such as “certified,” “compliant,” “safe,” “unbiased,” or “ready” unless the exact evidence and authority support the exact scope of that claim. Prefer: “mapped to,” “reviewed against,” “tested by,” “requires human review,” or “evidence missing.”
