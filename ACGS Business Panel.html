Practical techniques to standardize
  • Spec -> plan -> patch before broad implementation.
  • Check blast radius before changing a surface.
  • Use paired opposition or reviewer critique on important outputs.
  • Treat every output as a hypothesis until evidence supports it.
  • Anchor outputs to invariants: scope, tests, policies, and buyer-safe claims.

Buyer-Safe Copy Guardrails

Use evidence-scoped language and keep professional-review boundaries visible.

Allowed wording

  • ✓evidence-ready and replayable governed actions
  • ✓policy-governed action evidence
  • ✓audit-supporting and compliance-supporting evidence
  • ✓mapped to NIST AI RMF / EU AI Act concepts

Avoid wording

  • !Claims that the product alone satisfies external obligations.
  • !Claims beyond the evidence packet, policy versions, replay result, and review workflow.
  • !Claims that governed agents replace professional judgment in legal or clinical settings.
  • !Claims that evidence capture changes underlying AI behavior.

Human-review boundaries for legal and clinical verticals

  • !Legal flows should preserve lawyer review, matter context, citation discipline, and professional responsibility boundaries.
  • !Clinical flows should preserve clinician review, validation boundaries, and local policy review before deployment.
